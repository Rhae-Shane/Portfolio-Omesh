VIR Bike builds premium e-cycles. I interned as the person who put the brand on the web and on WhatsApp so a buyer did not have to call the workshop.

The site and chatbot were live in the window that led into a 7 Cr funding round. I will not pretend a landing page raised the round. I will say the company finally had a storefront and a support channel that did not die at 7pm.

## Company website

- Launched [virbike.com](https://www.virbike.com/) — catalog, specs, and test-ride requests
- Browse models without a PDF or a sales call
- Frontend in React + Tailwind; backend in Node + Express so the form and catalog could grow
- Pages that stay readable on a phone, because that is where most first visits happen

## WhatsApp chatbot

Most buyers already write on WhatsApp. Building a separate helpdesk would have been ignored.

- Product discovery, pricing, test-ride booking
- Order tracking after a deposit
- CRM handoff so a human can pick up a hot lead
- Payments in the same thread instead of a "we will send a link"

Chat: [message VIR Bike](https://api.whatsapp.com/send/?phone=9119440258&text=Hello%2C+I+need+assistance%21&type=phone_number&app_absent=0)

## Why both

The website is for someone who is still comparing. The chatbot is for someone who already wants a number, a slot, or a status. I worked across both so the catalog and the bot did not drift.
