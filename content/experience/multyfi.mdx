Multyfi is an investor app for equity, derivatives, and commodity ideas. I interned on the product that sits between research and the person who has to act on it — the screens, the feed, and the notifications.

## What I owned

- Stock recommendation strategies and the investor-facing surfaces that show them
- Real-time market data so a tip is not stale by the time someone opens the app
- Recommendation delivery and notification workflows across equity, F&O, and commodities
- Client flows that a 75K+ download base actually uses: watchlists, idea cards, and follow-through after a push

## Recommendations

Research is useless if it lands as a paragraph. I worked on how a tip is packaged: instrument, bias, entry, and why — then how it shows up in the feed versus a push.

- Idea cards that stay readable on a phone
- Strategy tags so a user can follow one desk without drowning in every product
- Delivery that does not double-fire when a job retries

## Live market data

- Wired live prices and candles into the recommendation views
- Cached the hot path so a feed scroll does not hammer the broker APIs
- Fallback when a candle source is down so the card still opens

## Notifications

- Workflows for equity, derivatives, and commodity desks
- Quiet hours and product filters so a commodity ping does not wake an equity-only user
- Deep links from the notification into the exact idea, not the home screen

The internship was long enough to see the same user come back after a bad fill. That is why the data path and the notification path had to agree.
